Working time and leave for a small Austrian team, with Arbeitszeitgesetz checks. One SQLite file, one binary that is both CLI and MCP server. Part of the back-office craft suite. Not payroll: no Lohnverrechnung, SV/LSt or ELDA.

Status: v0.1.1, Tier 1 (store + CLI + MCP, tested). No desktop window yet. Synthetic demo data only; not checked by a labour lawyer or Steuerberater.

  • How to use it day to day: HELP.md
  • What it is for and where it stops: docs/PRD.md
  • Every MCP tool: docs/TOOLS.md (generated, checked by a test)
  • Changes: CHANGELOG.md

What it does

  • Time: clock in/out or record entries (night shifts across midnight supported). No overlaps, one open entry per employee.
  • Week confirmation: an employee confirms an ISO week, then its entries are locked (in code and by SQLite triggers).
  • AZG check: more than 10 h a day (warning, needs an overtime arrangement), 12 h a day, 60 h a week, 48 h average over 17 weeks, 30 min break above 6 h (gaps of 10 min or more count), 11 h daily rest. Findings are recorded with minutes over/short; nothing is auto-corrected. Fixed data clears a finding but keeps it in the history.
  • Leave: vacation, sick, comp time, unpaid, other. Days counted Mon-Fri minus the 13 Austrian public holidays (Easter computed). Half days. Balance per year with pro rata entitlement in the start year, adjustments (carry-over), and an approval gate that refuses overdrawing unless forced.
  • Sick leave stores no reason (health data stays out, enforced in code and by the schema).
  • KV check: import your own KV minimum table (CSV) and compare each employee's gross pro rata to weekly hours. No KV figures ship with the app; they change every year.
  • Timesheet: monthly record per AZG section 26 (start, end, break, worked, absence, holiday, AZG flags), target vs worked balance, semicolon CSV for Excel.
  • Capacity: available hours per ISO week after holidays and approved leave; --emit sends capacity.changed to taskcraft over the craft-biz file bus.

Quick start

teamcraft demo-seed                         # 3 mock employees, fictional KV table, deliberate AZG findings
teamcraft violations
teamcraft leave balance --year 2026
teamcraft kv check --date 2026-11-09
teamcraft clock-in JM
teamcraft clock-out JM --break 30
teamcraft time add JM --date 05.11.2026 --start 22:00 --end 06:00 --break 30   # night shift
teamcraft leave request JM vacation 21.12.2026 --to 31.12.2026
teamcraft leave approve 3
teamcraft azg --from 2026-11-01 --to 2026-11-30
teamcraft timesheet JM 2026-11 --out D:\Temp\jm-2026-11.csv
teamcraft capacity 2026-W46 --emit         # taskcraft picks it up with `taskcraft inbox-drain`

KV CSV format (header optional, DE numbers): kv;group;valid_from;monthly_min;weekly_hours, e.g. IT-KV;ST1;01.01.2026;2.450,00;38,5.

Database: --db PATH, else $TEAMCRAFT_DB, else %LOCALAPPDATA%\teamcraft\teamcraft.db.

Product Requirements & Architecture Specification

What it is

Working time and leave for a small Austrian team, with Arbeitszeitgesetz checks, a KV pay floor check and team capacity for planning. Not payroll. Pure Rust, ArtCraft pattern. Suite plan: BACKOFFICE_CRAFT_SUITE.md, section 4.3.

Users

  • The employer of 1-10 people who must keep AZG records and approve leave.
  • Employees recording and confirming their own time (CLI today, egui later).
  • Agents over MCP: check AZG compliance, prepare timesheets, answer "who is available next week". Agent decisions are recorded with actor agent.

Jobs

1. Keep a working-time record that satisfies AZG section 26 and cannot be quietly changed after confirmation.

2. Spot AZG violations before an inspection does.

3. Handle leave with correct day counting and balances.

4. Make sure nobody is paid below the KV minimum.

5. Feed real availability into project planning (taskcraft).

Today (v0.1.0)

  • Employees (work fields only), clock in/out, manual entries incl. night shifts, overlap guard.
  • Week confirmation with trigger-enforced locks.
  • AZG engine: 10 h warning, 12 h, 60 h/week, 48 h average over 17 weeks, 30 min break above 6 h, 11 h rest; findings recorded, cleared but kept.
  • Leave in half-days against Austrian public holidays, pro rata entitlement, adjustments, approval gate, sick leave without reason.
  • KV import and pro rata check; monthly timesheet CSV with target and balance; weekly capacity sent to taskcraft (verified end to end).
  • 25 MCP tools (TOOLS.md, generated and test-checked).

Boundaries

  • No Lohnverrechnung, SV/LSt, ELDA, payslips.
  • No health data: sick leave stores day counts only.
  • No KV figures shipped; the user imports and owns the table.
  • Rules are the general AZG; not a substitute for advice from a labour lawyer or payroll office.

Gaps (honest)

  • No egui window; no employee self-service UI.
  • AZG: no KV-specific extensions (Gleitzeit, 4-day week), no ARG Sunday/night rules, no KJBG.
  • Urlaubsgesetz accrual in the first 6 months and the 30-day entitlement after 25 years are not modelled; part-timers are approximated on a 5-day basis.
  • Timesheet CSV only (no PDF).
  • Rules not reviewed by a professional; synthetic data only.

Next

1. egui app: clock panel, week view with AZG flags, leave calendar, approval inbox.

2. Labour-law review; Gleitzeit as an opt-in profile.

3. Timesheet PDF via pdfcraft.
